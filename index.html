About me

I'm a game developer from Buenos Aires focused on gameplay programming and level design in Unity and C#. I enjoy the part of development where code meets feel: tuning movement, building enemies that react intelligently, and shaping spaces that guide the player without words.

I've worked in teams from 2 to 8 people, always with version control in Git, and I've shipped games across different genres: shoot 'em ups, top-down arena shooters, an online co-op extraction shooter, an AI-driven 3D shooter and a first-person exploration game in Unreal Engine 5.

I hold Argentine, Spanish and Italian citizenship, which gives me EU work authorization, and I'm open to remote or relocation opportunities.
